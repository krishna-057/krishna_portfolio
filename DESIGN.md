# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World

Krishna's portfolio uses an AI lab notebook and model-evaluation console as its durable world: pale lab-film surfaces, black ink, copper warnings, teal signal traces, and restrained violet depth. It avoids the common dark-neon AI dashboard and the cream editorial portfolio default.

## Composition

The first viewport is split between a large written thesis and an instrument-like signal panel. Below it, work appears as ledger rows rather than generic cards, so projects feel like evidence entries. Capability content uses compact panels only where comparison is useful.

## Typography

System sans is used for clarity and speed. Scale, weight, and density create the character: large confident display text, tight navigation, compact technical labels, and readable body copy.

## Color Roles

`--lab` is the page surface. `--ink` carries primary text. `--film` and `--film-strong` create readable panels. `--copper`, `--teal`, and `--violet` are semantic accents used sparingly for signals, proof, and depth.

## Motion

Motion is limited to one authored arrival moment and one signal-line draw in the hero. Reduced-motion users keep the same hierarchy without animated movement.

## Interaction

Links and buttons have visible focus rings, minimum practical touch sizes, and simple hover states. Navigation remains predictable and section anchors are direct.
