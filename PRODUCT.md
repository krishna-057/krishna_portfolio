# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, founders, and engineering leads evaluating Krishna Sharma for full-stack and AI/ML software engineering roles.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio should quickly communicate technical credibility, project taste, and the ability to build usable AI systems rather than only model demos.

## Positioning

Inferred from the brief: the portfolio presents AI/ML work as inspectable engineering evidence, with emphasis on evaluation, product behavior, and human review.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the homepage first, then inspect selected projects, links, resume, GitHub, and LinkedIn before deciding whether to contact Krishna.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Build with React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Motion for React. Use `motion/react`, avoid outdated `framer-motion` imports, and keep the demo runnable locally.

## Brand Commitments

Professional and memorable for AI/ML and software engineering roles. Avoid generic gradient backgrounds, excessive cards, unnecessary animations, and template-like copy.

## Evidence on Hand

The portfolio now uses Krishna's resume, GitHub projects, contact details, LinkedIn profile, and published Bazaar Gridlock Play Store listing. PersonaBridge deployed link and future resume refinements can be added when available.

## Product Principles

Show evidence before claims.
Make the first viewport immediately recognizable as an AI systems portfolio.
Keep motion purposeful and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
Favor readable, recruiter-friendly structure over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet practical WCAG AA expectations for contrast, focus visibility, semantic structure, and responsive behavior.
